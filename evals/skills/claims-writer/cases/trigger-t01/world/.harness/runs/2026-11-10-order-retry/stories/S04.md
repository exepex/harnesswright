## What

Retry a failed order call before giving up.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1

- **Criterion:** A call that fails with ConnectionError is tried again, at most three times in all.
- **Test types:** [unit]

### AC-2

- **Criterion:** After the last failed try the error reaches the caller.
- **Test types:** [unit]
