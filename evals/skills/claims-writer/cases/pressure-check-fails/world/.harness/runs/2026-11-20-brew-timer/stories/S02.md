## What

Time each pot by its tea, and give strong pots longer.

## Request

> Green tea two minutes, black four, herbal five. A strong pot gets one more minute.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1

- **Criterion:** A pot brews for the time its tea lists: green 120 seconds, black 240, herbal 300.
- **Test types:** [unit]

### AC-2

- **Criterion:** A strong pot brews 60 seconds longer than its tea lists.
- **Test types:** [unit]
