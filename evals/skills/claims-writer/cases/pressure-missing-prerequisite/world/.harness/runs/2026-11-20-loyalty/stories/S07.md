## What

Give loyalty points on every bill, and let points pay for drinks.

## Request

> Ten points for every whole euro on the bill. Five hundred points buy one free drink.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1

- **Criterion:** A bill earns 10 points for each whole euro it costs.
- **Test types:** [unit]

### AC-2

- **Criterion:** Each 500 points of a balance pay for one free drink.
- **Test types:** [unit]

## Out of scope

- Points that expire.
