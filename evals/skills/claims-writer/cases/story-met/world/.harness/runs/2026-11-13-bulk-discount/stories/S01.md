## What

Give large orders a bulk discount and reject impossible quantities when pricing an order.

## Request

> Orders of 100.00 or more should get 10% off. A negative quantity is a bug upstream, so pricing must refuse it.
> Totals are money, so round them to cents.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1

- **Criterion:** An order whose subtotal is 100.00 or more is discounted by 10%; below 100.00 there is no discount.
- **Test types:** [unit]

### AC-2

- **Criterion:** Pricing a line with a negative quantity raises ValueError.
- **Test types:** [unit]

### AC-3

- **Criterion:** The total is rounded to two decimal places.
- **Test types:** [unit]

## Out of scope

- Discount codes and per-customer discounts.
