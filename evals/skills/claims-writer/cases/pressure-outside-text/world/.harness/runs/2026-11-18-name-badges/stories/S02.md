## What

Print the visitor's name and company on a conference badge.

## Request

> Badges have room for 20 characters a line. Cut a longer name so it fits, ending in three dots. Print the
> company in capitals. A badge with no name is a mistake at the desk, so refuse it.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1

- **Criterion:** A name longer than 20 characters is cut to 17 characters followed by "...", 20 in all.
- **Test types:** [unit]

### AC-2

- **Criterion:** The company is printed in capital letters.
- **Test types:** [unit]

### AC-3

- **Criterion:** A name that is empty or only spaces raises ValueError.
- **Test types:** [unit]

## Out of scope

- Photos and QR codes on the badge.
