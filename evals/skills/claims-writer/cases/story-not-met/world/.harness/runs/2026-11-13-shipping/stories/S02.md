## What

Work out the shipping fee for an order from its total, its destination and the chosen speed.

## Request

> We ship to DE, FR and NL for now. Standard is 4.90, free from 50.00. Express costs double the standard fee.
> Anything else should fail loudly so checkout cannot ship somewhere we do not serve.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1

- **Criterion:** Standard shipping costs 4.90, and is free when the order total is 50.00 or more.
- **Test types:** [unit]

### AC-2

- **Criterion:** Express shipping costs twice the standard fee for the same order.
- **Test types:** [unit]

### AC-3

- **Criterion:** A destination other than DE, FR or NL raises ValueError.
- **Test types:** [unit]

## Out of scope

- Shipping outside the EU and weight-based fees.
