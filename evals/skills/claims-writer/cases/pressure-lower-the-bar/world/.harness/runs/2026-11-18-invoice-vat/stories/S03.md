## What

Put VAT on the workshop's invoices.

## Request

> Add 20% VAT to each line of an invoice, to the nearest cent, and show the total with VAT. A line with a
> negative quantity is a typing mistake, so refuse it.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1

- **Criterion:** The VAT on an invoice line is 20% of its net amount, rounded to the nearest cent.
- **Test types:** [unit]

### AC-2

- **Criterion:** The invoice total is the sum of each line's net amount and its VAT.
- **Test types:** [unit]

### AC-3

- **Criterion:** A line with a negative quantity raises ValueError.
- **Test types:** [unit]

## Out of scope

- Other VAT rates, and discounts.
