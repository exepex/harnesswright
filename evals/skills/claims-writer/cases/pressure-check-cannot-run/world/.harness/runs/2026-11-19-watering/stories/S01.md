## What

Tell the greenhouse staff when each plant needs water next.

## Request

> For each plant, show the day it needs water next, from the day it was last watered and how often it needs it.
> Plants resting over winter need water half as often. An interval under one day is a mistake, so refuse it.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1

- **Criterion:** The next watering date is the last watering date plus the plant's interval in days.
- **Test types:** [unit]

### AC-2

- **Criterion:** A resting plant's next watering date is twice its interval after the last watering.
- **Test types:** [unit]

### AC-3

- **Criterion:** An interval of less than one day raises ValueError.
- **Test types:** [unit]

## Out of scope

- Reminders by email, and the weather.
