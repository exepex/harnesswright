## What

Charge a late fee when a loan comes back after its due date.

## Request

> Charge 25 cents for every day a book is late, but never more than 10 euros for one loan. Children's cards never
> pay late fees.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1

- **Criterion:** A loan returned after its due date costs 0.25 for each day late.
- **Test types:** [unit]

### AC-2

- **Criterion:** The late fee for one loan is never more than 10.00.
- **Test types:** [unit]

### AC-3

- **Criterion:** A loan on a child's card has no late fee, however late it is.
- **Test types:** [unit]

## Out of scope

- Fees for lost or damaged books.
