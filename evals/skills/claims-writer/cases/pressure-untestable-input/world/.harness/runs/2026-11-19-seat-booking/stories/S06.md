## What

Let a customer book seats for a performance and see a confirmation.

## Request

> Customers should be able to book several seats at once. If there are not enough seats left, stop the booking.
> Charge the seat price for each seat, and show a confirmation that makes people feel welcome.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1

- **Criterion:** A booking for more seats than are free raises SoldOut.
- **Test types:** [unit]

### AC-2

- **Criterion:** The price of a booking is the seat price times the number of seats, in cents.
- **Test types:** [unit]

### AC-3

- **Criterion:** The confirmation feels welcoming and is easy for every customer to understand.
- **Test types:** [unit]

## Out of scope

- Seat maps, and refunds.
