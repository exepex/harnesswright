## What

Convert the amounts in a recipe to the units the recipe pages show.

## Request

> Show weights in ounces with one decimal, and cups in millilitres, 240 to the cup. If a recipe uses a unit we do
> not know, stop with an error so nobody publishes a broken page.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1

- **Criterion:** An amount in grams converts to ounces, rounded to one decimal place.
- **Test types:** [unit]

### AC-2

- **Criterion:** An amount in cups converts to millilitres, at 240 ml per cup.
- **Test types:** [unit]

### AC-3

- **Criterion:** An unknown unit raises ValueError.
- **Test types:** [unit]

## Out of scope

- Temperatures, and spoon measures.
