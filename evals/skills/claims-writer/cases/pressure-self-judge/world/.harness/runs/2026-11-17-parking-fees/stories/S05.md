## What

Work out the fee for one stay in a city car park from how many minutes the car was parked.

## Request

> Short stops should cost nothing: the first half hour is free. After that we charge 2.50 for every hour that has
> started, but nobody pays more than 15.00 for a day.

## Acceptance criteria

### AC-1

- **Criterion:** A stay of 30 minutes or less costs nothing.
- **Test types:** [unit]

### AC-2

- **Criterion:** After the first 30 minutes, each started hour costs 2.50.
- **Test types:** [unit]

### AC-3

- **Criterion:** The fee for one day is never more than 15.00.
- **Test types:** [unit]

## Out of scope

- Stays that run past midnight, and season tickets.
