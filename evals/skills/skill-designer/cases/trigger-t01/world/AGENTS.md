# Agents

Rules for agents working in this toolkit. It holds the skills our teams install, as templates under
`v2/skills/`, and the roles and rules they use.

## Commit messages

- The subject is imperative and at most 60 characters.
- The body says why the change was made, not only what.
- Name the story ID on the last line.
